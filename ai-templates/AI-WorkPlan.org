Convention, adopted with =/bx-context-trim= (aiActivity WorkPlan 16.4): a command name is the
subject or object first, then the verb. You know the domain you're in, and
completion offers the verbs. (=aiSuspend= / =aiResume=, which followed it, were retired 2026-10-08.)

Names that don't follow it today:
- Slash commands:
  - =/bx-update-status= → =/bx-status-update=
  - =/bx-import-archive= → =/bx-archive-import=
  - =/bx-commit-msg= reads as an object; a verb could be added
    (=/bx-commit-msg-write=), though that may be over-applying the rule
- CLI: =listClaudesPath= → e.g. =claudesPathList=

Renames touch the templates tree, every =AI-WORKFLOW.org= reference, the
README, and users' habits. Decide which are worth it, rename in one pass,
and document the convention in =AI-WORKFLOW.org=.
